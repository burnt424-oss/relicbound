# RELICBOUND

A sci-fantasy tactical JRPG. *Final Fantasy Tactics meets Xenogears.*

The world of **Veyra**: a ~1990s-Earth civilization built over the Relic Strata —
ruins of the ancient Veyari, who fused machinery, biology, and consciousness.
Scattered across the map, the **Stillholds** refuse to evolve: feudal Japan,
Arthurian knights, steampunk cowboys, and everything in between.

You are **Kai**, a 22-year-old relic technician. The god-machine under the
cliffs just woke up — and opened its thousand-year-sealed doors for you.
Not because of a bloodline. Because you don't believe in gods.
