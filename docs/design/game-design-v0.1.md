# Sci-Fantasy Tactical JRPG — Game Design v0.1

Status: Approved by Brent on 2026-09-09.

## Core identity
Android-first, landscape, HD-2D-inspired top-down sci-fantasy JRPG with semi-open world exploration and medium-depth tactical turn-based combat.

## Exploration and world
- Classic top-down exploration with towns, dungeons, large regions, and stylized world map.
- Story/dungeon enemies appear in-world and battles occur seamlessly on the same map.
- World map uses random encounters so grinding remains deliberately available.
- Semi-open regions, hidden caves/ruins, vehicle-gated secrets, NPC/map clues.
- Light puzzles only.
- Vehicle progression culminates in a customizable mobile base.

## Story and tone
- Starts personal, develops into mystery and faction politics, then world-scale stakes.
- Opening: remote coastal research/monastic enclave studies a giant dormant relic structure beneath cliffs/seabed.
- Protagonist is a young relic technician; structure recognizes them for a non-bloodline reason, privately communicates, opens a sealed area, and a hostile faction arrives suspiciously fast.
- Ancient civilization fused machinery, biology, consciousness, and reality manipulation.
- Magic is real, but its boundary with ancient reality engineering remains ambiguous.
- Tone ranges from warm/adventurous to strange, tragic, creepy, epic, and absurd.

## Protagonist and party
- Named protagonist with authored sarcastic/skeptical/guarded personality; dialogue choices mostly shape tone.
- Gender presentation intentionally open.
- Protagonist begins as hybrid engineer and gradually gains reality-manipulation abilities.
- Opening companions: reckless competitive best friend and strange veteran relic hunter.
- Larger recruitable roster; 5–6 units deployed in combat.
- Story core plus optional/hidden recruits; mostly permanent roster.
- Full party travels narratively; only active field group is shown.

## Classes and progression
- Any character may use any class.
- Initial target 16–20 classes.
- Basic, advanced, side-path, faction, relic, boss, exploration, and hidden job unlocks.
- Visible main class paths; mysterious side jobs.
- Free class switching and free respec outside combat.
- Class mastery permanently unlocks selected cross-class skills/passives.
- Each character has class grids plus a smaller personal grid.
- Personal grids grow from levels, bonds/story, and relic/exploration discoveries.
- Standard level cap 99; all recruits gain XP; active units earn job mastery faster.

## Tactical combat
- Mostly invisible square grid.
- Slightly angled battle camera.
- Visible individual initiative timeline; abilities may manipulate turn order.
- Baseline turn economy: Move + Action; special skills can bend movement/action rules.
- No attacks of opportunity.
- Light facing: side/rear bonuses.
- Terrain and elevation matter; no explicit cover system.
- Moderate elevation baseline with select height-focused abilities.
- Statuses mix classic, tactical, and sci-fantasy effects.
- Lightweight elemental + terrain reactions.
- KO is normal defeat state; brutal battles may cause temporary injuries.
- Hybrid healing: MP/energy, items, limited emergency heals.
- Buildable signature ultimates; tactical play charges them faster.
- Shared Resonance/Momentum meter fuels manually activated bond and class combos.
- Tactical finishers for regular enemies; Break states for bosses.
- Bosses combine stats, mechanics, terrain, timeline, statuses, and phases.

## Difficulty and grinding
- World regions have fixed difficulty; grinding can overpower normal content.
- Optional challenges may scale selectively.
- Presets: Story, Normal, Tactical, Brutal.
- Accessibility settings can adjust undo leniency, encounter rate, text/animation speed, and XP progression.

## Equipment and economy
- Classic gear + modules + rare relic gear.
- Moderate crafting for upgrades, modules, relic tuning, consumables.
- Mostly unlimited inventory.
- Everyday currency comfortable; rare progression uses relic/faction/boss/exploration resources.

## Relationships and factions
- Flexible bonds: friendship, rivalry, mentorship, loyalty, found family, optional romance.
- Romance driven by chemistry/story rather than gender gates.
- Bonds unlock mechanical and narrative rewards.
- 3–4 major factions plus local groups.
- Reputation matters, but only a few clearly signaled decisions cause major branches.

## Android UX
- Android-first, landscape.
- Exploration: virtual stick + contextual action.
- Combat: tap-driven bottom command bar (Move, Attack, Skill, Item, Wait).
- Tap destination once to preview, tap again to confirm.
- Movement can be undone until another action commits; accessibility may extend undo.

## Presentation
- HD-2D-inspired pixel aesthetic with modern lighting, fog, particles, reflections, and relic glow.
- Mostly top-down exploration; slightly angled tactical battle view; selective dynamic camera moments.
- Flashy but efficient combat animation.
- Orchestral + synth + ambient soundtrack; experimental textures for relic/reality moments.
- Regional dynamically layered battle music and unique boss tracks.

## Endgame / NG+
- Complete main ending plus optional endgame, hidden jobs, relic zones, superbosses, rare gear, faction/companion content.
- NG+ carries nearly all progression and adds harder variants, new boss phases, exclusive relics/jobs/areas, and extra story revelations.

## Design principles
1. Old-school RPG charm.
2. Meaningful build freedom.
3. Tactical depth without excessive complexity.
4. Fast, readable Android interaction.
5. Rewarding exploration.
6. Grinding remains useful.
7. Characters stay distinct despite universal classes.
8. Mystery survives explanation.
9. Experimentation is not punished.
10. Modern convenience without losing retro identity.
