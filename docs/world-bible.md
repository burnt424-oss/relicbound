# World Bible (Brent's direct input, 2026-09-18)

## Tech level of the world
- Baseline civilization is roughly **1990s Earth** tech.
- **Ancient high-tech** (relic tech from the ancient civilization) exists alongside it — the "ehcs"/ancient tech.
- Key twist: **parts of the world refuse to evolve** — deliberately anachronistic regions:
  - **Feudal Japan** analogue (samurai-era culture, honor codes)
  - **King Arthur–style knights and horses** (chivalric medieval realm)
  - **Industrial cowboy-era steampunk** (frontier + steam industry)
  - "Everything in between" — other holdout cultures at various tech/culture levels.

## Design implications (agent notes)
- Each holdout region = distinct visual identity, enemy design, and combat flavor (knightly formations, steampunk firearms/explosives, samurai duelists).
- The "refusers" are natural faction material for the doc's 3–4 major factions (preservationists vs. exploiters of ancient tech).
- 1990s baseline keeps the world relatable; the anachronisms make the weirdness land.
- Vehicle/airship gating maps naturally: reaching isolated holdout regions requires new transport.
