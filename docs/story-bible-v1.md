# RELICBOUND — Story Bible v1 (agent-authored, 2026-09-18)

Brent said: take over, use anything, write the story. This is the canon. He can red-pen anything.

## The world
The world of **Veyra**. Baseline civilization is roughly 1990s Earth: television, highways, cassette futurism, small wars over oil and bandwidth. Underneath it, literally: the **Relic Strata** — ruins of the Veyari, an ancient civilization that fused machinery, biology, and consciousness into reality-engineering. Their machines still dream underground.

And scattered across the map are the **Stillholds**: entire regions that refuse to evolve. A feudal-Japanese archipelago that turned away surveyors with swords. An Arthurian highland of knights and horses that treats the 20th century as a rumor. A steampunk cowboy frontier running on coal, steam, and stubbornness. Nobody knows why the Stillholds froze — or why the modern world lets them.

## The opening (what the game builds first)
**Tidewatch Enclave**, a coastal research monastery built on cliffs above a dormant Veyari structure called the **Hollow** — a cathedral-sized machine asleep under the seabed. Our protagonist is **Kai**, 22, a relic technician: sarcastic, skeptical, allergic to mysticism. Kai's job is maintenance on machines everyone else prays to.

One night the Hollow wakes up. It speaks — not in words, in *adjustments*: doors that were sealed for a thousand years open for Kai specifically. Not because of bloodline. Because Kai is the first person in centuries who looked at a god-machine and said "you're just a machine." The Veyari feared worship more than war; they built their vaults to open only for disbelief.

Within the hour, a hostile faction arrives. Suspiciously fast. Somebody at Tidewatch called them.

## The party (opening)
- **Kai** — relic technician. Hybrid engineer who slowly gains reality-manipulation abilities. Sarcastic, guarded, kind under protest.
- **Mira** — Kai's best friend. Reckless, competitive, first into every sealed door. Fights like she has a spare life.
- **Soren** — a strange veteran relic hunter who shows up exactly when the Hollow opens. Knows far too much. Won't say how.

## The factions
1. **The Reclaimers** — corporate-state alliance strip-mining the Relic Strata. "The past is inventory." They arrived at Tidewatch within the hour.
2. **The Still** — the confederation of the Stillholds. They refuse the Relic Strata on principle: "we buried our gods once; we won't dig up someone else's." Not pacifists — knights, samurai, and rail-riders who will fight to stay frozen.
3. **The Tuned** — a cult/ideology growing in the 1990s cities: people who believe merging with Veyari consciousness is evolution. Charismatic, creepy, probably right about one thing.
4. **The Hollow itself** — the Veyari machines are not dead, and they have opinions. The fourth faction is the past, waking up.

## The spine (4 arcs, the 100-hour shape)
1. **Salt and Signal** — Tidewatch, the Hollow's opening, the Reclaimers' arrival. (The game's opening chapters.)
2. **The Stillholds** — each anachronistic region is a chapter: earn passage, take sides in the Reclaimer/Stilled conflict, recruit.
3. **The Tuning** — the Tuned make their move; the party learns what the Veyari actually were and why they vanished. (They didn't vanish. They *changed*.)
4. **Weave or Break** — the Hollow fully wakes. Endings branch on the party's bonds and what they believe the relics are: tools, gods, or ancestors. Multiple endings.

## Tone
Warm and adventurous at the campfire; strange, tragic, and creepy in the ruins; epic when the Hollow speaks; occasionally absurd (Mira). Mystery must survive explanation — the Veyari are never fully explained.

## Design anchors (from the approved doc, unchanged)
- HD-2D-inspired presentation; top-down exploration; slightly angled tactical battles.
- Any character, any class; 16–20 classes; class grids + personal grids; level cap 99.
- Move + Action; visible initiative timeline; no attacks of opportunity; light facing; terrain/elevation matter.
- Tap to preview, tap again to confirm; undo until commit.
- Resonance/Momentum meter for bond and class combos; buildable signature ultimates.
- Vehicles gate regions; progression culminates in a customizable mobile base; airships mid-game.
